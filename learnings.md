## Topic guidance

*   **Prioritize "What-if" scenarios:** Focus on topics that describe a hypothetical situation and its consequences, especially those involving human experience in extreme environments (e.g., "X में Y को क्या होगा?", "अगर Z हो जाए तो क्या होगा?"). These consistently show high engagement.
*   **Emphasize Earth's Extremes & Mysteries:** Continue exploring unique geological features, deep ocean phenomena, and unexplained terrestrial mysteries. These topics have demonstrated strong rewatchability.
*   **Simplify Space & Cosmos topics:** When covering space, aim for concrete, visually compelling aspects rather than overly abstract or theoretical concepts. Consider framing space topics as "what-if" scenarios (e.g., "बृहस्पति में गिरो तो..."). If a complex topic is covered, ensure it's broken down into easily digestible parts with strong visuals.
*   **Avoid topic redundancy:** While exploring related themes is good, avoid creating significantly longer versions of already covered topics unless a fresh, distinct angle is presented.
*   **Strictly adhere to hard rules:** No news events, real or named people, religion or mythology, politics or conspiracies.

## Hook & pacing guidance

*   **Immediate impact:** Open with the most counterintuitive or mind-bending fact within the first two sentences to quickly hook the viewer, especially for shorter videos.
*   **Optimal length for engagement:** While shorter videos (under 2 minutes) show exceptional relative retention, the channel should also aim for compelling content in the 4-6 minute range to allow for more depth, as some videos in this range (e.g., Mariana Trench) have performed well.
*   **Dynamic pacing:** Maintain a fast pace, especially for longer videos. The existing guidance of a new shot every 4–8 seconds is good, but for videos over 3 minutes, consider aiming for the lower end of that range (4-6 seconds per shot) or incorporating more visual variety and transitions to sustain attention.
*   **Mid-video re-hook:** For longer videos, a strong re-hook around the 30-40% mark is crucial to re-engage viewers whose attention may be waning.

## Thumbnail & title guidance

*   **Hindi is mandatory.**
*   **Curiosity-driven questions and statements:** Titles that pose a clear question ("क्या होगा?", "क्यों लगते हैं?") or make a startling claim ("हैरान है", "अदृश्य संसार") are effective.
*   **Numbers and specific metrics:** Incorporate numerical values (e.g., "11,034 मीटर", "11 किमी", "90 सेकंड", "10m, 20m, 50m") to quantify the extreme nature of the topic and create a strong curiosity gap.
*   **Emotional words and emojis:** Use impactful adjectives ("अजीब", "अद्भुत") and relevant emojis (e.g., 😱, 😨, 🌊) to convey emotion and visual interest.
*   **Strong contrast words:** Words like "अदृश्य", "गायब", "उल्टा" create intrigue.
*   **Concise and impactful:** Aim for titles that are clear, direct, and convey the core mystery or "what-if" scenario quickly.
*   **Thumbnail text:** 3–5 concrete nouns, one strong contrast word, reinforcing the title's curiosity gap.
