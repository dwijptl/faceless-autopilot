## Topic guidance

*   **Prioritize "What-if" scenarios (Category 3):** Focus on topics that describe a hypothetical situation and its consequences, especially those involving human experience in extreme environments (e.g., "X में Y को क्या होगा?", "अगर Z हो जाए तो क्या होगा?"). These consistently show high engagement and are a proven core strength.
*   **Emphasize Earth's Extremes & Mysteries (Category 1):** Continue exploring unique geological features, deep ocean phenomena, and unexplained terrestrial mysteries. These topics have demonstrated strong rewatchability and high AVD. This should remain the primary content family.
*   **Simplify Space & Cosmos topics (Category 2):** When covering space, aim for concrete, visually compelling aspects (probes, surfaces, descents) rather than overly abstract or theoretical concepts (white holes, quantum theory). Abstract cosmology has the channel's worst retention and should be capped strictly at ~1 in 8 long-forms, or even less, until retention improves for these topics. Consider framing space topics as "what-if" scenarios (e.g., "बृहस्पति में गिरो तो...").
*   **Avoid topic redundancy:** While exploring related themes is good, avoid creating significantly longer versions of already covered topics unless a fresh, distinct angle is presented that justifies the increased length and complexity.
*   **Strictly adhere to hard rules:** No news events, no real or named people, no religion or mythology, no politics or conspiracies.
