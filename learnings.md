## Hook & pacing guidance

*   **Strengthen long-form opening hooks:** The very low AVD from YouTube Search (0:00:27) indicates that the initial moments of long-form videos are not engaging viewers who arrive with specific intent. The opening must immediately deliver on the title's promise, establish the mystery, and present the core question compellingly, like a detective opening a new file.
*   **Develop distinct long-form narratives:** Do not simply extend successful short-form concepts. Long-form videos require a structured investigation, presenting evidence, weighing competing theories honestly, and building suspense towards an ending that clearly states what remains unexplained. The pacing should be methodical but visually dynamic, using documents, maps, and graphics to maintain interest.
*   **Leverage short-form success for long-form intros:** The high-impact, concise nature of successful Shorts can inform the *first 30-60 seconds* of a long-form video, but the subsequent narrative must expand with depth and evidence, not just repetition.
